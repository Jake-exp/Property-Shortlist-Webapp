# Property Shortlist

A small web app for ranking and comparing the houses on a shortlist: weighted scores, commute and fuel costs, market figures and a decision plan.

- Open the site on a phone and add it to the home screen to use it like an app. It works with no signal after the first visit.
- Everything you enter is stored in your browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, and this repository contains no property data.
- Use **Back up to a file** and **Restore from a file** to keep a copy or move your shortlist between phones.
- Photos you add to a property are shrunk and kept in the browser on that device only. They are never uploaded and are not part of a backup.

The whole app is `index.html`. `sw.js` and `manifest.webmanifest` make it installable and usable offline.
